# ArtifactFrame

A small app window, with a title bar and a body, around an Experience artifact that "plays" when its chapter is in focus.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />
<Controls />

## Use when

- An Experience chapter shows a miniature of the product it describes (`OuribankArtifact`, `ItauArtifact`, `OneGlobalArtifact`).
- The contents should animate in as the chapter comes into focus.

## Don't use when

- The content is real and must be read or used: the frame is hidden from assistive technology.
- It should show below `md`: the frame is `hidden md:block` on the site. These stories force it to show.

## Behavior

- The root is a `group` carrying `data-active`. Children react with the shared pieces in `artifact.style.ts`: `appear` is the base transition (`--i`, set with `stagger()`, delays each element by 90 ms) and `rise` fades and nudges an element in once `active` is true.
- `Inactive` shows the resting state: the title bar, with the contents faded out.
- Transitions are off under `prefers-reduced-motion` (`motion-reduce:transition-none`).

<Canvas of={Stories.Inactive} />

## Accessibility

- The whole frame is `aria-hidden="true"`: it is an illustration. Whatever it shows must be said in the slide's own text.
- Nothing inside may be focusable.
- The title uses `subtle` on `surface`, which meets 4.5:1.

## Do / Don't

| Do                                              | Don't                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Say in the slide's text what the artifact shows | Rely on the artifact to carry meaning  |
| Animate with `appear` + `rise` and `stagger()`  | Add one-off transitions per artifact   |
| Pass layout (width, margins) as `className`     | Restyle the chrome through `className` |
