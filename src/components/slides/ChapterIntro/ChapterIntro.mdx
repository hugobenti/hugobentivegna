# ChapterIntro

The opening copy of a chapter slide: a kicker, the title and a short body.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />
<Controls />

## Use when

- A case study chapter opens with a number, a headline and a sentence of context.
- Every chapter should read the same way: one component keeps the type scale in one place.

## Don't use when

- It is the case cover or the hero: they have their own, larger headline.
- You need more than three lines of copy: add them after it in the slide.

## Behavior

- Renders three siblings with no wrapper: the `p` kicker, the `h2` title and the `p` body.
- Each line enters with the slide (`reveal`), staggered 0, 1, 2. Lines you add after it continue at `stagger(3)`.
- The reveal needs the deck: inside Storybook the lines simply show.

## Accessibility

- The title is the slide's `h2`, so every slide keeps one heading level 2.
- Colors come from `text-fg`, `text-muted` and `text-subtle`, which meet AA on the page background.
