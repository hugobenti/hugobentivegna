# CaseCoverSlide

The slide that opens a case study: what it is, when, my role, and the chapters ahead.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />
<Controls />

## Use when

- A case study starts. Every case opens the same way, so the cover is one component fed with an `ICaseCover` from `src/content`.
- The case has a logo (`mark`) or a picture (`aside`) that belongs next to the headline.

## Don't use when

- It is the hero: it has its own, larger headline and the `enter` entrance.
- A chapter opens: use `ChapterIntro`.

## Behavior

- Everything enters with the slide (`reveal`), staggered: mark and kicker 0, title 1, body 2, meta 3, chapters 4. The aside enters with the body (2).
- With an `aside`, the copy sits on the left and the picture on the right from `lg` up; without one, the copy takes the width.
- A soft glow is drawn inside the slide's own box, so no hard line shows where two slides meet.
- The reveal and the slide's own layout come from `deck.css`, which Storybook does not load: here the lines simply show, in a stage that stands in for the slide.

<Canvas of={Stories.WithMarkAndAside} />

## Accessibility

- The slide is a labelled `<section>` (`slide.label`); the title is its `h2`.
- The meta items are a description list (`dl`) and the chapters an ordered list (`ol`), so counts and order are announced.
- The glow is decorative (`aria-hidden`). The mark and the aside must carry their own accessible name (`PixelArt` takes a `label`).
- Text uses `fg`, `muted` and `subtle`, which meet AA on the page background.

## Do / Don't

| Do                                                | Don't                           |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Keep the title to a sentence; it is balanced      | Put a paragraph in the title    |
| Give the mark and aside a text alternative        | Pass a decorative image unnamed |
| Keep three meta items: the grid has three columns | Add a fourth meta item          |
