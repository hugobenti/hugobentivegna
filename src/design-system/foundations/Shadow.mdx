# Shadow

Two shadows: a card lift for surfaces and an accent glow for highlights.

<Canvas of={Stories.Elevation} />

## Use when

- `shadow-card` to lift cards and popovers off the page.
- `shadow-glow` behind hero content or a highlighted element.

## Don't use when

- Shadows on every element: depth is a signal, not decoration.
- Relying on a shadow to separate content: add a border.

## Accessibility

- Shadows are decorative; a border (`line`) separates content for people who can't see them.
- The card shadow is dark: it only shows on dark surfaces such as these.

## Do / Don't

| Do                       | Don't                      |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| Card lift from the token | A new shadow per component |
