# PixelField

The PixelHB signature: amber pixels spilling in from an edge of the slide, held back at 40%. It replaces the blue radial glow; the grid is drawn by hand in `pixelField.data.ts`.

<Canvas of={Stories.TopRight} />
<Controls />

## Use when

- A slide needs a quiet signature in an empty corner: the hero, a case cover, the problem slide, contact, the 404.
- Once per screen: one `PixelField` on a slide, in a corner the copy leaves empty.

## Don't use when

- The spot is behind body text, a list or a control: the pixels would cut through the reading line. Pick another placement or leave it out.
- The slide already carries an illustration or a pixel drawing: two drawings compete.
- You want motion: the field never fades, moves or reacts; it is the end state for every visitor, reduced motion or not.

## Placement

- `top-right` (default): below the fixed header (`--hb-site-header`), against the right edge.
- `bottom-left`: the same corner mirrored on both axes, against the bottom-left corner.
- `centre-right`: halfway down the right edge.
- The parent must be positioned (`relative`) and clip its overflow; the field is `absolute` and ignores the pointer.
- 8 px a pixel on phones (224 × 176 px), 20 px from `lg` up (560 × 440 px).

## Accessibility

- Decoration only: the wrapper is `aria-hidden`, so the drawing has no name and no role for assistive tech, and nothing in it takes focus.
- One amber tone at 40% on the background: it carries no information, so it has no contrast requirement, and text never sits on it.
- No transition or animation classes: nothing moves, with or without `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Do / Don't

| Do                                    | Don't                          |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Put one field on a slide              | Stack two fields on one screen |
| Keep it in the corner the copy leaves | Let it run behind a paragraph  |
| Use the hand-drawn `FIELD` grid       | Generate pixels at runtime     |
